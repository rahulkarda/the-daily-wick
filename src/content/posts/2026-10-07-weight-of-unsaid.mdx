## The Idea

We often avoid difficult conversations, deferring the small, immediate friction of speaking an uncomfortable truth. We rationalize it as kindness, or discretion, or simply a lack of time. But the unsaid doesn't disappear; it accumulates. Every small omission, every polite silence in the face of needed clarity, adds a tiny, imperceptible weight. Eventually, this collection of unaddressed issues, unspoken resentments, or unclarified expectations forms a dense, invisible barrier.

Consider the colleague who consistently misses deadlines. A direct, early conversation is a moment of friction, yes, but it is contained. Without it, the frustration festers, projects stall, and the relationship itself strains under the weight of assumptions and unacknowledged problems. The cost of ease now is often complexity later.

## One Question

What truth, if spoken with care today, could prevent a much larger friction tomorrow?

## Today's Action

1. Identify one small, low-stakes instance where you've been deferring a clear communication.
2. Draft a single, concise sentence that addresses it directly and respectfully.
3. Deliver it today, acknowledging the brief discomfort as an investment in clarity.

## Go Deeper

Communication is rarely about eliminating friction entirely, but about choosing the right kind. Proactive, small friction is a maintenance cost. Reactive, large friction is a repair bill.
