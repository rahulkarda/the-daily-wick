## The Idea

We often equate ease with efficiency, especially in communication. It feels easier to sidestep a touchy subject, to leave an unsaid assumption hanging, or to soften a necessary critique into polite ambiguity. This immediate comfort, however, is a mirage. Each avoided conversation, each unaddressed tension, adds a tiny bit of relational "drag" – a subtle but persistent friction that accumulates over time. Like grit in a machine, these small resistances eventually seize up the gears of trust and cooperation. The initial discomfort of a direct, honest conversation is a short-term friction that prevents the far greater, more damaging resistance of resentment, misunderstanding, or a relationship that slowly grinds to a halt.

## One Question

What small, uncomfortable truth are you postponing that, if spoken, could clear the air for good?

## Today's Action

1. Identify one area in a key relationship (work or personal) where a truth remains unsaid.
2. Draft a single, clear sentence stating the core issue without blame.
3. Schedule a brief conversation today to share that sentence, aiming for understanding, not resolution.

## Go Deeper

Friction is not always a problem to be solved; sometimes it's the necessary force that shapes and strengthens. The friction of truth, though initially uncomfortable, is the forge in which genuine connection is made.
