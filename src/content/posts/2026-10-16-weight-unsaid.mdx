## The Idea
We often avoid uncomfortable conversations, believing we're preserving peace or sparing feelings. This impulse to ease the immediate moment, however, often creates a deeper, more entrenched form of friction in our relationships. The small, unaddressed comment, the polite evasion, the unspoken expectation – each adds a tiny, imperceptible weight. Over time, these accumulate, making genuine connection feel like pushing against a heavy, unseen barrier. What felt like a short-term gain in comfort becomes a long-term cost in intimacy and trust. The resistance builds not from what was said, but from the cumulative pressure of what was not.

## One Question
What small, uncomfortable truth, if spoken kindly today, could prevent a greater friction tomorrow?

## Today's Action
1. Identify one minor point of friction in a relationship (personal or professional) that you’ve been avoiding.
2. Frame the unspoken thought as a gentle, direct question or observation, not an accusation.
3. Initiate the conversation today, aiming for clarity and understanding, not necessarily immediate resolution.

## Go Deeper
Ease is seductive, but it often defers costs. Sometimes the most loving act is to introduce a small, necessary friction now, to prevent a much larger one from hardening later. It is a choice to build with honesty, not just comfort.
