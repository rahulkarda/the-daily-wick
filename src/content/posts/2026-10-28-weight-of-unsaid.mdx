## The Idea
We often avoid difficult conversations, opting for the immediate ease of silence over the discomfort of speaking our truth. This avoidance, however, creates a subtle, insidious friction. It’s the constant, low-level hum of resentment, the unacknowledged tension that subtly warps relationships, and the quiet internal debate that drains mental energy. This 'unsaid' doesn't disappear; it accumulates. Like silt in a river, it builds up, making the current sluggish and eventually blocking the flow entirely. The short-term relief of silence is a deceptive bargain, traded for a heavier, more complex problem down the line.

## One Question
What unsaid truth is generating more friction by its silence than it would by its expression?

## Today's Action
1. Identify one small, low-stakes truth you've been avoiding saying to someone. This isn't about confrontation, but clarity.
2. Draft a simple, direct sentence that expresses it without blame or accusation.
3. Practice saying it out loud to yourself a few times. Don't send it yet, just prepare the path.

## Go Deeper
The friction of a necessary conversation is an investment. It’s an upfront cost that prevents the much larger expense of misunderstanding, distance, and regret. Choose the small friction now, to avoid the heavy resistance later.
